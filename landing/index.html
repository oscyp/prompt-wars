GOOD TO KNOW.

Is Prompt Wars free to play?

Core battles and all five archetypes are free. Optional purchases include cinematic videos, cosmetics and additional move suggestions. Purchases do not add scoring bonuses.

Do I need to be good at writing prompts?

You can build a move by choosing an action, intention and approach. Write your own whenever you want to take a different direction.

Does every battle include an AI video?

Every completed battle gets a free result reveal. Generated videos are optional, depend on available allowances and capacity, and may require credits.

Where will I be able to play?

Prompt Wars is coming soon to iOS and Android. Choose your platform to get a launch notification.
