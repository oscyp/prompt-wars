# Composer: Action → Intention → Approach → Your move

Status: implementation verified, backend deployed and both internal client artifacts delivered. iOS 1.3.3 (18) is VALID / IN_BETA_TESTING; Android 1.3.3 (9) passed exact-artifact installation and startup. Full native acceptance and reliable AI-bank availability remain unconfirmed; the limitations below are open acceptance gaps.

## Scope

The approved separate-review design now has three creative decisions, explicit Next/Back, full expanded scene context and exact review with a revision-bound 600 ms hold. The reducer preserves visited branches and separate manual/builder buffers; draft v4 migrates existing text without interpreting prose. Review has no change/edit/Undo controls, and successful autosave is silent. Legacy single/Bo3 behavior is preserved.

AI banks use additive composition version 3 with 81 reachable complete moves across the three types. Offline starters remain explicit after eight seconds or immediately on failure. Free custom adaptation has six delivered contexts per round, three attempts per context, shared attempt limits and a private lease ledger. Existing free and purchased banks receive exact-set, no-charge enrichment. Pricing, scoring, ranking, bots and the existing client contract remain unchanged.

[Sanitized measurements](../audits/2026-10-07-composer-four-view/backend/README.md#real-provider-evidence-and-limitations) retain no generated text or credentials. This timing is consistent with the configured 45-second generation deadline, but function logs were inaccessible, so the exact provider failure cause is **not confirmed**. The round completed using explicit starters. The authorized Bo3 then completed through manual writing, with a drawn second round and final defeat 0–2. The six-call ceiling applied to the synthetic benchmark; ordinary automatic free-bank preparation in this one authorized Bo3 was separately bounded to its three rounds, with no retries, purchases or custom adaptations.
