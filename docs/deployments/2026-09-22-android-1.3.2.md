RevenueCat received the Google test event at **14:45 UTC** after Google Play's notification service account received topic-scoped Pub/Sub Publisher access.

Both subscription base plans are also **Active**, backwards compatible, and available in **174 countries / regions**. RevenueCat reports all six Android products **Published** as of approximately **15:55 UTC**.

The Console creation form rejected the valid ASCII IDs `monthly` and `annual` in both the in-app Browser and Chrome. After explicit user approval of Google API Explorer's Terms and account access, the official `monetization.subscriptions.batchUpdate` call returned HTTP 200 and created both draft base plans using those unchanged IDs. Regional prices were then saved and both plans activated through Play Console. Monthly costs US $9.99 / PLN 46.99; annual costs US $59.99 / PLN 279.99, with converted prices in other regions. This bypasses the creation-form issue without changing app or RevenueCat product IDs.
