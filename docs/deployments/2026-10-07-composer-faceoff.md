## Implemented boundaries

- Face-off starts the three free owned banks when Build is selected. Cache/in-flight recovery happens first; missing banks use separate authenticated requests, at most three in parallel. Each result can appear without waiting for the slowest generation. Next does not generate or buy anything.
- Explicit starters after eight seconds/error remain available. A legacy bank whose free Approach enrichment fails stays preserved, including its purchase metadata, while complete starters can be chosen explicitly. Late results do not replace active selections.
- Action reroll uses the existing endpoint. New `reroll-move-step-suggestions` handles exact-parent Intention and Approach rerolls at the configured price (normally 1 credit; existing test waiver 0). Confirmation, write-before-charge journals, shared limits, moderation, lease fencing, atomic delivery and one owed refund remain mandatory. This client no longer invokes free custom-fragment completion; that older endpoint is unchanged.
- Private paid-step operation/attempt tables and service-only RPCs ship in `20261007154542_paid_composer_step_rerolls.sql`. The migration also accepts the effective zero test price while preserving older test-client catalogue-price confirmations. Expired charged Action recovery now retains its operation identity when generation/rate-blocked, or fences and refunds once when the round/prompt/context makes delivery impossible. Delivered retries still return the original result and price.
- Draft v5 stores Face-off and visited branches. Manual/build buffers stay separate. Older custom or unstructured drafts preserve exact text/type in freestyle; their displaced buffers remain reachable through a rare migration-only Earlier drafts section. Swapping archived versions preserves the current one.
- Reduced type-control spacing and stable row measurement avoid the Android blank area and clipped Finisher label; large text stacks these controls.
