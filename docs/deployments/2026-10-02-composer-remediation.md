# Composer remediation — backend and client delivery

The backend is deployed, **iOS 1.3.3 (16) is available in internal TestFlight**, and **Android 1.3.3 (7) is available as a verified test APK**. Android7 includes the native-discovered keyboard correction and passed installation and signed-out startup checks. Android6 and iOS15 are superseded intermediate artifacts. Remaining native acceptance limits are recorded below; no public store release occurred.

## Shipped behavior

- Suggestion moderation validates and classifies titles, actions, intentions and all complete action/intention pairs independently. Incomplete classifier responses and the shared 60-second timeout fail closed. The existing fictional-combat policy is unchanged.
- Delivery checks the current round, deadline and owner's submitted prompt atomically. Late paid delivery refunds once; retrying a successful purchase retains its original result and price. Worker fencing, lease renewal, consistent parent-first locks and attempt accounting have concurrency coverage.
- New experience-2 Bo3 series pin private bot policy 2. Legacy-experience and single-format rows retain policy 1. Server-only seeds, the 45-tactic catalog and immutable round choices are prepared before submission, including human rounds that can later need a bot. Existing assigned series keep policy 1.
- The composer starts with nine authored actions, grouped by type, and three intentions after selection. The full catalog has 135 actions and 405 intentions. Scene inspirations filter locally. Selection, replacement, cancel, Undo and v3 drafts preserve type together with text.
- Entering Build prepares three separate free AI banks. Incoming ideas require an explicit view change; purchases are single-type and display separately. Writing alone does not trigger v2 generation. Confirmation contains the exact prompt and type.
- Authoring origin is analytics only, and never enters judging. Appeal availability distinguishes a read failure from ineligibility and refreshes after a conflict.

Ranked remains on experience 2 and the current judging policy. Combat, asynchronous deadlines, ratings and per-type suggestion pricing are preserved. Human calibration and the pilot remain separate quality work, without a restored ranked release gate.
