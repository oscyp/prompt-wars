# Native purchase review screenshots

Captured on 22 September 2026 from the existing authenticated Prompt Wars iOS development app on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator, iOS 26.5. The app loaded the current production routes from the local Metro server, including the monthly/annual wallet changes. These are original 1206 × 2622 PNG screenshots from `simctl io screenshot`, with no resizing, compositing, or price replacement.

- `ios-credit-packs-1206x2622.png`: all four credit packs (10, 30, 80, 200 credits) and their live RevenueCat offering prices.
- `ios-plus-monthly-annual-1206x2622.png`: Plus benefits, both subscription purchase controls, and their monthly/yearly renewal disclosures.

The app displays its store-provided localized USD strings: `1,99 US$`, `4,99 US$`, `9,99 US$`, `19,99 US$`, and Plus `9,99 US$/month` / `59,99 US$/year`. These captures verify the real app presentation and current RevenueCat offering; they do not prove an Apple sandbox or production purchase completed.

The app was restarted to clear an old development error toast. The Expo floating development button was hidden only for the capture launch using `-EXDevMenuShowFloatingActionButton NO`. No purchase buttons were pressed, no balances or store configuration were changed, and no build was performed.
